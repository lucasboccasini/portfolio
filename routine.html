Tips for your plan

Sleep and recovery

Leave room for rest between runs. Try a repeatable bedtime cue and choose a plan that does not regularly cut into the sleep you need.

Read the sleep guide →

Food and hydration

Use familiar foods and notice what feels comfortable. Consider weather, route length, and your individual needs when planning fluids.

Read the nutrition guide →

Before you head out

Check the weather, choose comfortable layers, and reserve a few minutes to warm up at an easy pace.

Read the pre-run guide →

Run and progress

Start with an effort you can repeat. If you are new, walk-run intervals are one option. Keep recovery time and change your plan gradually.

Read the running guide →

Busy weeks

Choose a minimum version of your habit, such as a short walk or laying out your shoes. A flexible plan can restart after a missed day without guilt.

Read the lifestyle guide →

Remember: A plan should support your life. Adjust it for your needs, experience, weather, and energy. If you have a health condition, injury, or concerning symptoms, ask a qualified professional for personal guidance.
